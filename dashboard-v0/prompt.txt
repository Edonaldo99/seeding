## 7. Ejemplo de Prompt completo

Create a modern admin dashboard called "Apple Music Admin".
Use a dark Apple Music style. San Francisco font. Rounded components.

Divide the screen into five visual sections:

1. Card: "Top Artists - Last 30 days"
   Use a horizontal bar chart.
   Example data:
   Bad Bunny: 185k seconds
   Ricardo Arjona: 86k seconds
   Taylor Swift: 77k seconds
   Feid: 76k seconds
   Metallica: 74k seconds

2. Card: "Top 10 Songs Guatemala - Last 7 days"
   Use a table:
   Columns: Title, Artist, Plays, Listeners

3. Card: "Premium Zombies - Last 30 days"
   Use a big KPI element:
   "16 Premium Zombies"
   Then a small table: Username, Country, Email

4. Card: "Reggaeton Demographics"
   Use a bar chart with age ranges:
   15: 13
   20: 22
   30: 19
   40: 22
   50: 4

5. Card: "Heavy Users - Bad Bunny"
   Use a table:
   Username | Country | Subscription | Distinct Songs
